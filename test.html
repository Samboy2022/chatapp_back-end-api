Frequently asked questions

How secure is my financial data?

We use bank-level 256-bit encryption and are SOC 2 Type II certified. Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit, and we never store your banking credentials.

Most customers are fully operational within 48 hours. Our dedicated onboarding team guides you through every step.
